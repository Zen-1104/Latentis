# PRESENTATION_SPEC.md — The Deck

Owned by `docs-ppt-engineer`. This is a **specification for slides**, not the slides. It fixes each slide's
single claim and the artifact path that backs it, so that building the deck is transcription rather than
invention — and so that when a number changes, exactly one place needs editing.

## 1. Constraints that override slide-design instinct

1. **Every number carries its source artifact path.** In the deck this is a footnote in 10 pt. A number without
   one is **deleted**, not footnoted later (`RT-001`).
2. **No figure is drawn by hand.** Every chart is generated by a script into `reports/figures/` and referenced.
   A hand-drawn chart cannot be regenerated when the number changes, and the number will change.
3. **`SYNTHETIC` appears on every slide showing data.** Bottom-left, always, unremovable in the template
   (INV-3).
4. **Status vocabulary exactly** (`CLAUDE.md § 4`). `Verified` names its test. `Measured` names its artifact.
   A `Target` is written as "target". `QG-DOC-02` audits the deck against `FINAL_STATUS.md`.
5. **Banned phrasings** (`docs/GLOSSARY.md § E`): "AI-powered", "confidence: N %", "accuracy" for the screening
   task, "predicts failure", "guaranteed", "validated" applied to the physics model, and anything implying ISRO
   operational data — "ISRO component data", "flight hardware readings", "field data".
6. **At Phase 0 no slide may contain a result.** Slides 10 is a placeholder frame with the metric *names* and
   their artifact paths, and nothing where the numbers go. Filling it is `T-707`, after `T-406`.

## 2. Audience and budget

Eight minutes of speech, twelve slides, interleaved with the live demo (`docs/DEMO_SCENARIO.md`). The panel is
mixed: at least one reliability or parts-qualification practitioner who will recognise PAT immediately, and at
least one generalist who will not. Every slide therefore has to work for both — the domain sentence first, the
plain-language sentence second, never the reverse.

**Two things win this and nothing else does:** that the approach is grounded in a standard the expert already
respects, and that every number on screen can be checked. Feature count is not on the list.

## 3. Slide-by-slide

### S01 · Title

- **Claim:** none. Identification only.
- **Content:** LATENTIS · SIH26170 · AI-Driven Anomaly Detection in Component Burn-In & Screening · ISRO ·
  team name · the one sentence from § 9.
- **Evidence path:** n/a.
- **Narration (10 s):** the problem-statement title verbatim, then the one sentence. Nothing else.

### S02 · The escape

- **Claim:** a part can pass every absolute limit and still be the one that fails in orbit.
- **Content:** one lot's leakage distribution with the datasheet maximum drawn as a hard line, the lot median
  near the left, and one part sitting far out in the tail yet clearly *inside* the line. The whole argument is in
  the picture: the existing gate is the vertical line, and the interesting part is nowhere near it.
- **Evidence path:** `reports/figures/lot_distribution_<part>.svg`, generated by `scripts/make_figures.py` from
  `reports/DEMO_PARTS_<tag>.json`. Values quoted in text come from
  `reports/METRICS_<tag>.json` and the part's own investigation payload.
- **Narration (45 s):** state the mechanism — burn-in exists to precipitate infant-mortality failures; a part
  whose parametric behaviour is abnormal *for its own lot* is the classic latent-defect signature; absolute
  limits are blind to it by construction because they are set for the worst acceptable part, not for this lot.
- **Phase 0 state:** figure absent. The slide exists with the axis labels and the caption; no numbers.

### S03 · What the standards already do — and what they do not

- **Claim:** the two normative practices we build on exist, are named, and are **not our invention**.
- **Content:** two rows. Row 1 — AEC-Q001 Part Average Testing / DPAT: lot-relative limits from robust
  statistics; already normative in automotive parts qualification. Row 2 — MIL-PRF-38535 delta limits: reject a
  part whose parameter *moved* more than a permitted delta across burn-in; normative, and **retrospective** —
  it needs the post-burn-in reading.
- **Evidence path:** `research/DOMAIN_RESEARCH.md § 4` and `§ 5`, with the confidence marker shown as-is
  (AEC-Q001 is MEDIUM; `HUMAN-002` open).
- **Narration (50 s):** "Module A is Dynamic PAT. We did not invent it and we are not claiming it. What we add is
  the multivariate layer, the attribution step, and — the actual contribution — making the delta-limit criterion
  *predictive*." Saying this out loud is what makes the extension credible; a practitioner who recognises PAT and
  hears it claimed as novel stops listening.
- **Note:** the MEDIUM confidence marker stays visible. It reads as rigour, not weakness.

### S04 · The contribution, compressed

- **Claim:** four things, each falsifiable.
- **Content:** exactly four lines, no more:
  1. **Standards-grounded, not invented** — Module A *is* AEC-Q001 DPAT; Module B makes MIL-PRF-38535's
     delta-limit criterion available 144 h before it is measurable.
  2. **A bound, not a guess** — rejection reads a distribution-free conformal upper bound, so "false negatives
     are catastrophic" becomes a parameter (`α`) instead of a sentiment.
  3. **Every number is re-derivable** — and we publish the rate at which that succeeds, from an audit that has
     no access to our own computation code.
  4. **The corpus is built to be hard for us** — sub-linear drift so linear extrapolation is not a straw man,
     plus decoy strata that must *not* be flagged.
- **Evidence path:** each line footnotes the slide that expands it (S05–S09) and its `FINAL_STATUS.md` capability
  row (`C-01`, `C-05`, `C-13`, `C-15`).
- **Narration (40 s):** read the four lines. Do not elaborate — the next five slides are the elaboration.

### S05 · Module A — abnormal for its own lot, and *why*

- **Claim:** the flag is lot-relative, decomposed per parameter, and attributed to a cause.
- **Content:** the DPAT limit formula rendered from the formula registry (`median + k · (IQR / 1.35)`), the
  robust z, the multivariate `D²` with its **exact additive** per-parameter contributions as a bar chart, and the
  attribution verdict: `PART` / `SOCKET` / `ZONE` / `TESTER`.
- **Evidence path:** `reports/figures/contribution_<part>.svg`; formula strings from
  `backend/core/formulas.py` via the investigation payload; `reports/METRICS_<tag>.json § attribution`.
- **Narration (55 s):** the contribution decomposition is exact, not a heuristic attribution — `D²` is a sum of
  terms and we show the terms. Then the attribution point: a socket with a measurement offset makes every part in
  it look anomalous, and scrapping those parts is the expensive mistake this step prevents. `RT-006` tests it with
  a bad part in a bad socket, which is the case that separates real attribution from a plausible one.

### S06 · Module B — two points, and the honest limit of two points

- **Claim:** with `v0` and `v24` only one per-part parameter is identifiable, and we say so instead of fitting a
  curve per part.
- **Content:** the decomposition on one line — `value_i(t) = v0_i + A_i · Φ_g(t)`, `Φ_g(24) ≡ 1`,
  `A_i = v24_i − v0_i` — then the forecast curve for the demo part with the **naïve linear extrapolation drawn as
  a dashed line, always**, even where it is unflattering.
- **Evidence path:** `reports/figures/forecast_<part>.svg`; `models/drift/DRIFT_SPEC.md § 3`;
  `reports/METRICS_<tag>.json § tail_mae` beside `§ baseline_tail_mae`.
- **Narration (55 s):** "Two points cannot identify a curve. Anyone who tells you they fit a per-part trajectory
  from two readings has fitted noise. We split it: the *shape* is learned across the group, the *amplitude* is the
  one thing the two readings do identify. Linear extrapolation is the special case `Φ(168) = 7`, so the baseline
  is inside our model family — which is why we can always show it."
- **Rule:** the dashed baseline is never removed from this figure, whatever it shows. `frontend-engineer`
  non-negotiable 7 and this slide are the same commitment.

### S07 · The bound, not the point — and one dial for risk

- **Claim:** the rejection decision reads `Û168(1−α)`, not `V̂168`, and `α` is the only knob.
- **Content:** the point forecast, the conformal upper bound, the limit, and `predicted_margin` measured against
  the **bound**. Beside it, the posture dial at three settings showing the flagged count changing.
- **Evidence path:** `reports/METRICS_<tag>.json § coverage` (empirical coverage with its Clopper–Pearson
  interval, per Mondrian level), `models/CONFORMAL_SPEC.md § 2`, `RT-005`.
- **Narration (50 s):** a point forecast crosses the limit for about half the parts that will actually cross it —
  under "false negatives are catastrophic" that is the wrong side of a coin flip. The bound has finite-sample,
  distribution-free coverage under exchangeability. Then the honest half: exchangeability is exactly what a
  drifting test floor breaks, which is why there is a guard — and the guard *widening the bound* is on the next
  slide's screenshot, not hidden in a log.
- **Note:** never say "guaranteed" unqualified. The guarantee is conditional and the condition is named.

### S08 · Explainability with a number attached

- **Claim:** every displayed value can be recomputed from its own payload by a process that cannot see our code —
  and we publish the success rate.
- **Content:** one `TracedValue` shown expanded (value, unit, `formula_id`, inputs, parameters, `dataset_hash`),
  the rendered formula beside the rendered sentence a QA inspector reads, then the two audit numbers:
  `rederivation_rate` and `verdict_agreement`.
- **Evidence path:** `reports/METRICS_<tag>.json § rederivation_rate` / `§ verdict_agreement`, produced by
  `RT-007`; `docs/PROVENANCE_SPEC.md § 3`.
- **Narration (55 s):** "Every team here will say their model is explainable. Ask them for the number. `RT-007`
  runs in a separate process with no access to `backend.core`, takes each value on screen, re-evaluates its
  formula from its own recorded inputs with a restricted evaluator, and compares. That rate is in the metrics
  file. Anything below 1.0 blocks the release — it is not a score we are proud of, it is a gate."
- **Why this slide matters most:** it converts the softest evaluation criterion in the problem statement into the
  hardest number in the submission.

### S09 · The corpus, and why it is built against us

- **Claim:** the data is synthetic, labelled synthetic, and deliberately unfavourable to our own method.
- **Content:** the strata table `S0`–`S5` with the decoys marked **must not flag**; the Escape Set defined as
  "passes every absolute limit and is truly defective"; the three physically separate split files; and the
  sub-linear `Φ` statement — `Φ(168) < 7`, so naïve linear extrapolation over-predicts and our advantage has to be
  earned.
- **Evidence path:** `reports/DATASET_PROFILE.md` (generated, not written), `data/DATASET_SPEC.md § 4`,
  `TEST-GEN-004` (Escape Set non-empty — a release blocker).
- **Narration (50 s):** "This is synthetic data and we are not going to pretend otherwise; we have no ISRO data
  and we have not invented any. What we did instead is make the corpus hard: the drift is sub-linear, which
  favours the baseline over us; there are lots with a legitimate process shift and sockets with measurement
  offsets that a naïve detector will flag and ours must not. Our false-positive rate is measured against those,
  not against easy negatives — so it is not comparable to anyone else's, and it is higher than it would be on a
  friendly corpus."
- **Rule:** the words `validated` and `realistic` are not used about the generator. It is "constructed from
  published mechanism models", with `HUMAN-005` open for expert review.

### S10 · Results

- **Claim:** whatever the artifact says. Nothing else.

### S11 · Limitations, volunteered

## 5. Banned in the deck — the specific list

"AI-powered" · "confidence: N %" (our scores are not calibrated probabilities) · "accuracy" for the screening
task (degenerate at this defect rate — see `D-012`) · "predicts failure" (we forecast a *parameter*, and apply a
standard's criterion to it) · "guaranteed" without its condition · "validated" for the generator · "real-time"
(we mean sub-second on one part) · "99 %" or any round number not read from an artifact · "ISRO component data" /
"flight hardware readings" / "field data" (INV-3).

Replacement phrasings live in `docs/GLOSSARY.md § E`. Use them verbatim; they were chosen to be defensible under
a follow-up question, which is where loose phrasing actually costs.

Each slide is paired with the question it invites, so the answer is prepared rather than improvised. The full set

| Slide | The question it invites | Where the answer lives |

question has no honest answer, it goes into `SIH_JUDGING_STRATEGY § 4` **with** the honest answer — including
"unknown" where that is the truth.

## 8. Submission-form text

Reproduce these verbatim. They are written to survive being read out of context by someone who has not seen the
deck, and they contain no number, because no number is measured yet.

### One sentence

> We implement the aerospace/automotive standard for lot-relative screening, make its delta-limit criterion
> predictive with a distribution-free bound, and prove every number on screen is computed rather than composed.

### One paragraph

> Burn-in screening rejects components against absolute datasheet limits, so a part that is statistically
> abnormal for its own lot — the classic latent-defect signature — passes and reaches the payload. LATENTIS adds
> two modules. Module A implements AEC-Q001 Dynamic Part Average Testing: robust lot statistics produce dynamic
> limits inside the absolute ones, a multivariate stage decomposes the anomaly exactly per parameter, and an
> attribution stage separates a bad part from a bad socket, zone or tester — so good parts are not scrapped for a
> fixture fault. Module B takes the 0 h and 24 h readings and forecasts the 168 h value, then applies
> MIL-PRF-38535's parametric delta-limit criterion to a distribution-free conformal **upper bound** rather than to
> a point estimate, because false negatives are catastrophic and a point forecast is a coin flip at the limit.
> Because two observations cannot identify a per-part trajectory, the forecast factors into a group-learned shape
> and a per-part amplitude, and the naïve linear extrapolation is displayed beside every forecast as the special
> case it is. Every displayed value carries its formula, inputs and parameters, and an independent audit process
> with no access to the computation code re-derives each one and publishes the success rate. All data is
> synthetic, labelled synthetic everywhere, and deliberately constructed to be unfavourable to our own method.

### One page

Assemble in this order, with no additions: the paragraph above; the four contribution lines from S04; the strata
and Escape Set definition from S09; the limitations list from `FINAL_STATUS.md § Known Limitations` (all fifteen,
not five — a form has room); the status counts from `FINAL_STATUS.md § Capability status`; the two commands from
S12. Nothing is rewritten for the form — the whole point is that the same sentences hold up in every venue.

## 9. Pre-flight checklist

Run before the deck is considered final (`T-707`). Every item is a fail, not a warning.

1. `grep` the deck for every digit. Each one resolves to a path under `reports/`. `RT-001` does this
   mechanically; do it by eye as well, because a number inside an image is invisible to the scan.
2. `SYNTHETIC` present on every slide showing data, and inside every figure's own footer.
3. Every banned phrase from § 5 absent — including in speaker notes, which get read aloud.
4. Capability wording matches `FINAL_STATUS.md` word for word. `Verified` names its test.
5. S10 contains no placeholder, no rounded "≈", no "TBD", and no zero standing in for a pending number.
6. S11 matches the current `FINAL_STATUS.md § Known Limitations`.
7. Every figure regenerated at the tagged commit — a figure from an earlier run is a `STALE` number with a chart
   around it.
8. The MEDIUM-confidence marker on the AEC-Q001 citation is still visible unless `HUMAN-002` has closed.
